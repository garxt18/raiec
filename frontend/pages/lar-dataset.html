How a rate gets here

Tender vetted and approved
Non-Scheduled items extracted
Lowest rate kept as the LAR

Approving an estimate records the rate for every Non-Scheduled item in it. Where the item is already known, the lower of the two rates is kept, so the benchmark only ever moves down.
