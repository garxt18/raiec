About

What is RAIEC?

RAIEC — the Railway Automated Intelligent Estimate Checker — is an internal tool for North Western Railway's Civil & Construction unit. It reads IREPS tender estimate PDFs, structures every line item, and checks each quoted rate against the official IRUSSOR and CPWD DSR schedules and the Last Accepted Rate (LAR) dataset — then supports an officer approve / reject workflow that keeps the LAR growing.

Automated extraction

Schedules, line items, rate breakups and Non-Scheduled items are parsed straight from the tender PDF — no manual data entry.

Accurate rate matching

Item-code matching against DSR & IRUSSOR, a fuzzy-description fallback, and LAR matching for NS items — with a clear OK / WARN / FAIL verdict.

Decisions that compound

Every approved estimate feeds accepted rates back into the LAR dataset, so each new check is grounded in a richer history.

The organisation

North Western Railway

North Western Railway is one of the zones of Indian Railways, with its headquarters at Jaipur, Rajasthan. Formed in October 2002, the zone covers Rajasthan and adjoining parts of Haryana, Punjab and Gujarat, and is organised into four operating divisions.

RAIEC was built for the zone's Civil & Construction unit, where every proposed work — a new station building, a road under bridge, track renewal — is tendered through IREPS and must be vetted before it is awarded.
